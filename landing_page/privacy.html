Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 15, 2025

Welcome to Kairos. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you create an account with Kairos, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Profile information (name, optional profile picture)
  • Authentication credentials (encrypted)

1.2 Journal Content

Your journal entries are the core of our service. We collect and store:

  • Text entries you create
  • Voice recordings you submit (stored as audio files)
  • Photos and images you attach to entries
  • Metadata associated with entries (timestamps, categories)

1.3 Usage Data

To improve our service and provide personalized insights, we collect:

  • Journaling frequency and streak information
  • Feature usage patterns
  • App performance data and crash reports
  • Device information (model, operating system version)

1.4 AI-Generated Content

When you interact with our AI assistant:

  • Your prompts and conversations are processed to generate responses
  • AI-generated insights and summaries are stored with your account
  • Pattern analysis data across your journal entries

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

2.1 Core Service Delivery

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Kairos app
  • Enable AI-powered journaling conversations
  • Generate personalized insights across life dimensions
  • Track your journaling streaks and achievements
  • Sync your data across multiple devices

2.2 Communication

  • Send you important service updates
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Send optional motivational reminders (if enabled)

2.3 Analytics and Improvement

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve features
  • Monitor app performance and fix technical issues
  • Develop new features based on user needs

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Where We Store Your Data

Your data is stored using industry-standard cloud infrastructure:

  • Local storage on your device (Isar database for offline access)
  • Secure cloud storage via Firebase services
  • Data centers located in [specify region]

3.2 Security Measures

We implement robust security measures to protect your information:

  • End-to-end encryption for data transmission
  • Encrypted storage of sensitive information
  • Secure authentication via Firebase Authentication
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and monitoring

3.3 Offline Mode

Kairos works offline by storing data locally on your device. This data is encrypted and automatically syncs to the cloud when you're connected to the internet.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 We Do NOT Share Your Journal Content

Your journal entries, voice recordings, and personal reflections are private. We do not sell, rent, or share this content with third parties for marketing purposes.

4.2 Limited Disclosure

We may disclose your information only in these specific circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party services that help us operate the app (e.g., Firebase, cloud storage, AI processing). These providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulation.
  • Safety and Security: To protect the rights, property, or safety of Kairos, our users, or others.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred (you will be notified).

4.3 AI Processing

To provide AI-powered insights and conversations, your journal content is processed by AI services. This processing:

  • Occurs in secure, encrypted environments
  • Is used solely to generate personalized insights for you
  • Does not involve human review of your content
  • Is not used to train AI models on your personal data

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

5.1 Access and Portability

  • Request a copy of all your data
  • Export your journal entries in a standard format

5.2 Correction and Update

  • Update your profile information at any time
  • Correct inaccurate data

5.3 Deletion

  • Delete individual journal entries
  • Request complete account deletion
  • Upon account deletion, all your data is permanently removed within 30 days

5.4 Opt-Out

  • Disable push notifications
  • Opt out of analytics data collection (may limit some features)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@kairos-app.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Kairos is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will delete such information.

7. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have different data protection laws. By using Kairos, you consent to such transfers. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Specifically:

  • Journal Entries: Retained indefinitely until you delete them or close your account
  • Account Information: Retained until account deletion
  • Usage Data: Aggregated and anonymized after 12 months
  • Deleted Data: Permanently removed within 30 days of deletion request

9. Third-Party Services

Kairos integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, storage, and cloud functions
  • Google Sign-In: Optional authentication method
  • AI Services: For generating insights and conversations

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them:

  • Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
  • Sending you an email notification
  • Displaying a prominent notice in the app

The "Last updated" date at the top of this policy indicates when it was last revised. Your continued use of Kairos after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

Email: privacy@kairos-app.com
Support: support@kairos-app.com
